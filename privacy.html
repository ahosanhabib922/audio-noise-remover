Privacy Policy

Audio Noise Remover · Last updated 4 September 2026

The short version. Your recordings never leave your phone. All audio processing happens on the device. We have no servers that receive your audio, and we do not ask you to create an account.

Who we are

Audio Noise Remover is an Android app developed by Ochinpur. In this policy, "we", "us" and "the app" refer to Ochinpur and its app. You can reach us at support@ochinpur.com.

Audio you record or import

Recordings you make, files you import, and every enhanced or edited version stay in the app's private storage on your device. Noise reduction, effects and editing all run on your phone's processor. We do not upload, copy, read or analyse your audio, and no third party receives it through the app.

Audio leaves your device only when you choose to send it. That happens when you use Share, or when you use Save Audio to write a file into your Music folder. In both cases you pick the destination and the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions we ask for

  • Microphone. Used only while the Recorder screen is open, to capture the audio you are recording. It is not used in the background.
  • Media and files. Used when you import an audio file you selected, and when you save a finished file into your Music folder. We only touch the files you choose.

Information that is collected

We never ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we hold no account for you. Some Google services used by the app do process limited technical data.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. Tells us which features are used and where people give up, so we know what to improve. What it sends is described in the next section.
  • Google Play Billing. If you buy a subscription or the lifetime unlock, Google processes the payment. We never see your card, address or full name. We receive only whether a valid purchase exists for your Google account, so the app can unlock Premium.
  • Firebase Remote Config. Used to change prices, offers and feature settings without an app update. It sends a random installation identifier, the app version and your country to Google. It is not linked to your name or your recordings.
  • Google Play Integrity. Used to check that the app is a genuine copy installed from Google Play, which protects paid features from tampering. It sends a device attestation to Google.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics in detail

The app records a small set of anonymous usage events through Google Analytics for Firebase. They are tied to a randomly generated app instance identifier, not to you.

  • What we send. Which screen was opened, which effect or editing tool was applied and which preset was chosen, how long a recording was in seconds, whether a file was saved or shared and to which kind of app, when the Premium screen was opened and from which button, and whether a purchase succeeded, was cancelled or failed.
  • What Google adds automatically. Device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate country derived from the network address.
  • What is never sent. Your audio, any part of it, file names you type, the contents of your library, your contacts, your location beyond country, and any advertising identifier.

We use this only in aggregate. It is not used to profile you, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers. The identifier resets if you clear the app's data or reinstall the app.

Crash reports

If you have turned on automatic crash and usage reporting in your Android settings, Google Play may share aggregate crash statistics with us. Those reports contain no audio and no personal identifiers.

Advertising

The app contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your recordings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete any recording from the Home screen menu. Uninstalling the app removes every recording, setting and cached file it created, except files you saved to your Music folder yourself.

Purchase records are held by Google Play, not by us. You can review or cancel a subscription in the Google Play app under Payments and subscriptions.

Your rights

We hold no name, email address or account for you, so there is no personal profile for us to export or erase. The usage events described above are anonymous and cannot be traced back to an individual, which also means we cannot pick yours out to delete them. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app resets the identifier they were attached to, and they are deleted automatically after two months.

For data held by Google in connection with your purchases, contact Google Play support or use the privacy controls in your Google account. If you have any question about your data, write to us at support@ochinpur.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@ochinpur.com.
