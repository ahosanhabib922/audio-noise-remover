Audio Noise Remover

Audio Noise Remover is an Android app that removes background noise from voice recordings and lets you edit them, entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to create.

Get it on Google Play

By Ochinpur · Last updated 20 November 2026

What it does

  • Record or import a voice memo, lecture, interview, podcast take or any other audio file.
  • Remove background noise: the noise reducer learns the room from the quiet parts, then takes out hiss, hum and fan noise while leaving the voice intact.
  • Make the voice clearer with compression, a limiter, EQ, pitch, echo and reverb, each with a before-and-after preview.
  • Edit the recording: trim, split, fade, remove silence, change speed or reverse it.
  • Save or share the cleaned audio as a WAV file to your Music folder or straight to another app.

Questions people ask

How do I remove background noise from a voice recording on Android?

Open the recording in Audio Noise Remover, tap Noise, pick a strength and listen to the preview. When it sounds right, apply it and save. The whole process happens on the phone and takes a few seconds for a typical voice memo.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Noise reduction, every effect and every editing tool run on the device, so the app works in flight mode. A connection is only used for ads on the free version and for buying Premium.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. The app contains no code that sends audio off the phone and there is no server to receive it. Recordings stay in the app's private storage until you save or share them yourself.

What kinds of noise can it remove?

Steady background noise: hiss, hum, fans, air conditioning, traffic rumble and room tone. It is built for speech, so it works best on voice memos, lectures, interviews, sermons, meetings and podcast takes. Sudden sounds such as a door slam or another person talking are not removed.

Can I clean up a recording I made in another app?

Yes. Import any M4A, MP3, WAV, OGG or FLAC file up to 10 minutes long, including voice notes saved from a messaging app, and clean it the same way as a recording made in the app.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and use, with ads. The free version includes recording, importing, every effect with manual control, and the Trim, Split and Reverse tools. Premium removes all ads and adds the named presets, the Fade, Silence and Speed tools, and automatic enhancement. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does it work on video files?

No. It is an audio app. It opens and saves audio files only.
